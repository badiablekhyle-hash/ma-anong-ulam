Features

Meal Recommendations

The Meal Recommendation feature speeds up daily meal planning by providing automated dish suggestions for Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner. Designed to remove the hassle of deciding what to cook, the feature allows users to easily shuffle through alternative recommendations whenever they prefer different meal options.

Meal Cards

Easily check dish details with compact recipe cards that have everything you need,
A step by step instructions, prep time, ingredients, and recipes. This makes choosing your next dish super easy!

Chef's Corner

Chef’s Corner is a feature that serves as a dedicated hub for your favorite meals, allowing instant access to your top recipe choices without having to shuffle through options. It features a built-in search tool so you can quickly locate specific dishes by name. Users have complete control over their curated collection, with the ability to easily add dishes via the meal card heart icon or remove them anytime to keep their personal list up to date

How to use Meal Planner
    1. Click add a plan
    2. Click the calendar button and select a date
    3. Choose a meal category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)
    4. Enter the name of the meal
    5. Type the ingredients in the box and the click the plus button.
    6. Upload a photo of your meal
    7. Add a description and/or instructions (optional)
    8. Click Submit.
    9. To find your meal plan, select the planned date on the calendar above the cards.
    10. You can click the meal card you created if you want to edit, delete, check the ingredients and check the instructions.
    11. To add more meals, click "Add a plan" and repeat these steps. Note: You can only have one meal per category on a given date.
    To add a new one , you must remove the existing meal.
