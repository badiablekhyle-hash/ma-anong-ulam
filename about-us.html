ABOUT US

This Meal Planner is a simple web app designed to help students and individuals plan their meals easily and conveniently. We created this app to make meal planning less stressful, especially for those who have a busy schedule and sometimes don't know what to prepare or eat.

Our Meal Planner allows users to organize their meals, plan what to eat for the day or week, and keep track of their meal choices. It aims to save time, reduce food waste, and encourage better meal planning.

We believe that planning meals does not have to be complicated. With our simple and user-friendly web app, organizing your meals can be quick, easy, and convenient.
